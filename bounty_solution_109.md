# Youth Protection Rules

**Last Updated:** 2024-05-20  
**Version:** 1.0

## 1. Age Eligibility
- Users must be **13 years or older** to create an account on Swapatron5000.
- Users under 18 require **explicit parental or guardian consent** to use the platform.
- We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we discover such data, it will be deleted immediately.

## 2. Parental/Guardian Responsibilities
- Parents/guardians are responsible for monitoring their minor’s activity on the platform.
- We encourage parents to:
  - Review the **Privacy Policy** and **Terms of Service** with their child.
  - Set up parental controls on devices used to access Swapatron5000.
  - Discuss online safety and appropriate behavior with their child.

## 3. Data Privacy for Minors
- We collect only the minimum necessary data for account creation and functionality.
- Minors’ data is **never sold** or shared with third parties for advertising purposes.
- Parents/guardians may request access to, correction of, or deletion of their minor’s data by contacting [privacy@swapatron5000.org](mailto:privacy@swapatron5000.org).

## 4. Community Safety & Moderation
- All user-generated content is subject to moderation.
- **Prohibited behavior** includes:
  - Harassment, bullying, or hate speech.
  - Sharing personal information (e.g., full name, address, school, phone number).
  - Inappropriate or explicit content.
- Reports of unsafe behavior can be submitted via the **Report** button on any user profile or post.
- Our moderation team reviews all reports within **24 hours**.

## 5. Communication Guidelines
- Users under 18 **cannot** initiate private messages with users they do not already follow.
- All private messages are logged and subject to review for safety violations.
- Users may block or mute other users at any time.

## 6. Enforcement
- Violations of these rules may result in:
  - Temporary suspension of account privileges.
  - Permanent account termination.
  - Reporting to law enforcement in cases of illegal activity.
