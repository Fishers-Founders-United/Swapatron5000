# IoT Swapatron 5000

**A free electronics parts swap box, built by the community. Box one is planned for Fishers, Indiana. Any town can hatch its own.**

**Take a part. Build something. Pass it on.**

Meet Swapatron. It is a box. It eats spare electronics parts, and it gives them to whoever needs them next.

> **Nothing is decided yet.** Swapatron has not hatched. Its shape, its rules, its name, and its dates are all open. **You can start contributing right now. Nothing holds it back.** We are also gathering people, and the first meetup is proposed for a Wednesday evening, **January 13, 2027**, online on the Mutiny19 Discord. [Start here](docs/start-here.md), [count yourself in](../../issues/new?template=count-me-in.yml), or [join the founding team call](docs/founding-team.md).

## The idea

Think of a neighborhood book box, but for dev boards, sensors, wire, and connectors. The first Swapatron is planned for Fishers, Indiana, outside the Indiana IoT Lab. It is the HQ model and the primary test bed for the whole idea. The community would design it, build it, feed it, and look after it.

It is not only for Fishers. The plans are open, so any town can [hatch its own](docs/your-swapatron.md).

You feed it the parts in your drawer. It passes them on to someone who will build with them. (Yes, "passes". We are not explaining further.)

## Who it is for

- **New to electronics?** Swapatron shares. Take free parts, with a note on what each one is and what to build first.
- **Been building for years?** Swapatron is hungry. Feed it your spare parts, and start someone off with what you know.

## Youth Protection Rules

To ensure a safe, welcoming, and secure environment for all participants—especially minors and young makers—the IoT Swapatron 5000 community adheres to the following youth protection guidelines:

1. **Supervised Participation:** Minors are encouraged to participate in building, swapping, and learning. However, any physical meetups, workshops, or build sessions involving children under 13 must be attended and supervised by a parent, legal guardian, or designated responsible adult.
2. **Safe Spaces and Conduct:** All community interactions (online via Discord, GitHub, and at physical box locations) are strictly subject to our code of conduct. Harassment, bullying, or inappropriate content of any kind will not be tolerated.
3. **Physical Safety around Electronics:** Parts distributed through Swapatron include safe beginner components (such as low-voltage microcontrollers, LEDs, wires, and sensors). High-voltage components, hazardous materials, damaged lithium-ion batteries, and sharp tools are strictly prohibited from being deposited in the swap boxes.
4. **Privacy and Data Protection:** We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without verifiable parental consent. Online usernames and community contributions should follow standard privacy best practices.

## Six open doors

**Start right now. No gates, no dates, no permission.** Almost nothing is built, so every door is open. Each one has a first step that takes about fifteen minutes. [Start here](docs/start-here.md).

| Door | What you do |
|---|---|
| 1. **Point your AI at it** | Paste one line into your AI assistant and let it help you contribute. [How](docs/ai-contribute.md) |
| 2. **Add to the ask list** | Say what you need for a project, or fill someone's ask. [Asks](ASKS.md) |
| 3. **Start a drawing** | Sketch the box, a part of it, or the site. [Drawings wanted](hardware/designs/WANTED.md) |
| 4. **Start UI and UX** | Design how people find and use the box. [Screens wanted](ux/WANTED.md) |
| 5. **Design an IoT integration** | Cameras, inventory, solar, safety, security, and discovery. [Integrations](integrations/README.md) |
| 6. **Fill the kit wishlist** | Pledge or lend parts that contributors need to prototype. [Kits](bom/KITS.md) |

You do not have to build any of it. These doors are for starting.
