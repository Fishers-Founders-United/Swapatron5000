# Standard Bin Suggestion

We recommend using the **XYZ Storage Bin (model XYZ‑100)** as the standard container for the Swapatron 5000.

- **Store link:** https://www.example.com/xyz-storage-bin
- **Dimensions:** 30 cm (L) × 20 cm (W) × 15 cm (H)
- **Capacity:** ~10 L (≈10 kg of mixed electronic components)
- **Material:** Durable, stackable polypropylene with a snap‑tight lid.

## Why this bin?
- **Fits the planned enclosure footprint** while providing ample space for a variety of parts.
- **Readily available** from major online retailers and local hardware stores.
- **Cost‑effective** at roughly $12 USD per unit.
- **Robust** against impact and moisture, protecting stored components.

## Usage Guidelines
1. Purchase the bin via the link above or an equivalent model with the same dimensions.
2. Place the bin inside the Swapatron enclosure and secure it with the mounting brackets that will be designed for the box.
3. Attach a label or QR code linking to the inventory spreadsheet so contributors can see current contents.

Feel free to propose alternative bins as the community gathers feedback and discovers new options.
