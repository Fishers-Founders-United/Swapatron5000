# Front Box Sign Design

## Overview

This document outlines the design specifications for the sign that will be placed on the front of the Swapatron5000 product box. The sign should be clear, legible, and consistent with the Swapatron brand identity.

## Dimensions

- **Overall size**: 200 mm × 100 mm (width × height)
- **Margin**: 10 mm on all sides
- **Text area**: 180 mm × 80 mm

## Color Palette

- **Background**: #FFFFFF (white)
- **Primary text**: #003366 (dark navy)
- **Accent**: #FF6600 (orange)

## Typography

- **Primary font**: Montserrat Bold, 48 pt
- **Secondary font**: Montserrat Regular, 24 pt

## Layout

1. **Top section** – Swapatron logo centered, 60 mm high.
2. **Middle section** – Product name “Swapatron 5000” centered, 48 pt.
3. **Bottom section** – Tagline “Swap. Share. Sustain.” centered, 24 pt.

## File Format

- Export as PDF and PNG at 300 dpi.
- Include a vector version (SVG) for future scaling.

## Usage

Place the sign on the front of the box, centered horizontally and vertically, ensuring the 10 mm margin is respected.

## Notes

- Avoid any text that is not part of the official branding.
- Ensure the sign is printed on high‑quality matte paper.
