# Youth Protection Rules

## Purpose
The purpose of this document is to define the policies and procedures that all contributors, maintainers, and users of **Swapatron5000** must follow when the platform is used by or interacts with children and youth (individuals under the age of 18). These rules ensure compliance with applicable laws, protect minors' privacy and safety, and provide clear guidance for anyone working with kids.

## Scope
These rules apply to:
- All code, features, and services that collect, store, or process personal data of minors.
- Any community interaction (forums, chat, support) involving minors.
- Volunteers, staff, and third‑party partners who have direct contact with children while using the platform.

## Legal Framework
| Region | Relevant Legislation | Key Requirements |
|--------|----------------------|------------------|
| United States | **COPPA** (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) | Obtain verifiable parental consent before collecting personal information from children under 13. Provide clear privacy notices. |
| European Union | **GDPR** (General Data Protection Regulation) – Article 8 | Obtain parental consent for children under 16 (or lower age as permitted by member state). Ensure data minimisation and the right to erasure. |
| Canada | **PIPEDA** & provincial child‑protection statutes | Similar consent and privacy obligations. |
| Other jurisdictions | Local child‑protection and privacy laws | Follow the most stringent applicable requirement. |

## Core Safety Principles
1. **Age Verification**
   - Implement robust age‑verification mechanisms for any feature that may collect personal data from users under 18.
   - Use non‑intrusive methods (e.g., date‑of‑birth entry, third‑party verification services).
2. **Parental/Guardian Consent**
   - Obtain verifiable consent from a parent or legal guardian before any data collection from children under the applicable age threshold.
   - Store consent records securely and make them retrievable for audit.
3. **Data Minimisation**
   - Collect only the data strictly necessary for the intended functionality.
   - Avoid storing sensitive personal data (e.g., location, health information) unless absolutely required and consented.
4. **Transparency**
   - Provide a child‑friendly privacy notice written in plain language.
   - Offer a separate adult‑focused privacy policy that references the child‑specific provisions.
5. **Secure Storage & Access Controls**
   - Encrypt all personal data at rest and in transit.
   - Restrict access to youth‑related data to personnel with a legitimate need‑to‑know.
6. **Retention & Deletion**
   - Define clear retention periods (e.g., delete data after the child reaches adulthood unless they opt‑in to retain it).
   - Provide easy mechanisms for parents/guardians to request data deletion.

## Operational Guidelines for Staff & Volunteers
- **Background Checks**: All individuals who will have direct contact with minors must undergo background screening consistent with local regulations.
- **Training**: Mandatory child‑safety training covering:
  - Recognising signs of abuse or neglect.
  - Proper communication etiquette with minors.
  - Reporting procedures for suspicious activity.
- **Supervision**: Activities involving minors must be supervised by a qualified adult at all times.
- **Code of Conduct**: Enforce a strict code of conduct that prohibits any form of harassment, exploitation, or inappropriate contact.

## Reporting & Incident Response
1. **Immediate Reporting**: Any suspected abuse, data breach, or policy violation involving a minor must be reported to the designated **Youth Safety Officer** within 24 hours.
2. **Investigation**: Conduct a prompt, confidential investigation while preserving evidence.
3. **Notification**: If a breach affecting a minor’s data occurs, notify the affected families and relevant authorities as required by law.
4. **Remediation**: Implement corrective actions, update policies, and provide additional training as needed.

## Development Checklist for Youth‑Related Features
- [ ] Does the feature collect personal data from users under 18?
- [ ] Is age verification implemented?
- [ ] Is verifiable parental consent obtained and stored?
- [ ] Are data minimisation principles applied?
- [ ] Are encryption and access controls in place?
- [ ] Is the privacy notice updated to reflect the new feature?
- [ ] Has the feature been reviewed by the Youth Safety Officer?
